# Round three: session and spells mutation sample

## Method and preregistered predictions

This continues the PF-102 one-byte mutation method on the frozen `pkg/session` and `pkg/spells` populations. The source base for both populations and every probe was Part 1 commit `aa867bf4dfa25c0fcafe337c11071d56ed7aee40`. Each package contributed 40 randomly drawn functions using the seeds and literal-selection procedure recorded in [PREDICTIONS.md](PREDICTIONS.md); the first ASCII letter of each chosen output literal was changed to `Q` (or `Z` when it was already `Q`). Predicted scenarios were frozen in [run/probes.tsv](run/probes.tsv) before each census.

Round two’s population collector was corrected for one-argument selector calls. Regenerating its historical population at `5e9556a8789c4125bed8913d34d16e6f2ed68fac` added **2 function rows** (`Dream` and `DreamTravelFn`) and **29 eligible literals**; the frozen round-two sample and denominator remain unchanged. The round-three population files and their hashes are recorded in [PREDICTIONS.md](PREDICTIONS.md).

Every mutation received one full census at source base `aa867bf4dfa25c0fcafe337c11071d56ed7aee40`. Each uncaught probe received the required single full recheck. The retained first-pass and recheck results show baseline-red scenarios, not infrastructure-only failures; per-probe fail rows and run paths are recorded in [run/probes.tsv](run/probes.tsv).

## Catch results

Both preregistered package forecasts were **57.3%**, with an 80% posterior predictive interval of **17–28 catches out of 40** (22.9 expected). The raw mutation-specific catch counts fell inside that interval for both packages. Wilson intervals are 95%; the raw rates are the reported results.

| Package | Mutation-specific catches / 40 (raw rate; 95% Wilson) | Prediction |
|---|---:|---|
| `pkg/session` | 24/40 = 60.0% (44.6%–73.7%) | 57.3%; 17–28/40 (22.9 expected); above midpoint and inside interval |
| `pkg/spells` | 20/40 = 50.0% (35.2%–64.8%) | 57.3%; 17–28/40 (22.9 expected); below midpoint and inside interval |

Across packages, **44/80** mutations produced a mutation-specific failure (55.0%; 95% Wilson 44.1%–65.4%). The other **36/80** were uncaught baseline-red misses and remain in their package denominators. No infra-excluded or selected catch rate is reported.
