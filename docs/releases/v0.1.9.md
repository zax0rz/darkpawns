Dark Pawns v0.1.9 is a week of checking the port against the original line by line, and fixing what didn't match. Most changes are small. Taken together, spells, fights and monsters now behave the way they did in 1994, down to who a monster attacks first and how many swings you get the round after a scroll lands.

**Spells**

- **No-magic rooms work again.** The port read the wrong room flag, so spells cast in a no-magic room went off anyway. They now fizzle with the original's words: "Your magic fizzles out and dies." for casters, and "Your will fades, disturbed by an unseen force." for psionics (#2401).
- **Spells that scale with the caster use the caster's level**, as in the original: lay hands, meteor swarm and circle of summoning. Mirror image clones are level 1 again (#2327, #2337).
- **Good and evil mean what they meant.** Spells that care about alignment use the original's thresholds (350 and -350), not "above or below zero" (#2322).
- **Hellfire knocks its victims to sitting**, not resting (#2301).
- **Reagents are matched by the exact item, not its name**, so a renamed or look-alike item no longer counts as a component (#2321).
- After a damage scroll hits, the next round of the fight has the right number of attacks (#2325).
- Group spells reach your followers in the original's order, and the zen staff heals as it did (#2340, #2343).
- Spell messages are capitalized as the original printed them, and identifying a wand or staff shows the right details (#2341).

**Fights and monsters**

- **Monsters that are bashed or stunned can still flee and cast** when the original let them, and monster script timers no longer stop their attacks (#2308).
- **Aggressive monsters pick targets the way the original did**, including the alignment-based aggression quirks and choosing the newest arrival in the room first (#2346, #2314).
- **Hidden attackers fade into view** the way they did, and each onlooker sees the name they should see (#2326, #2316, #2318).
- The parry line reaches the right people (#2347).
- Monster sounds, dreams, and a long list of special monster behaviours now print the original's lines (#2271, #2351, #2352, #2363, #2372).
- The spiked-weapon check, bite and mindlink messages match the original (#2356, #2360, #2365).

**Commands**

- **`palm`** now works like `get` with the quiet flag, as in the original. The port's invented "You palm the item skillfully" path is gone (#2383).
- **`subdue`** and **`strike`** follow the original's rules and messages, including strike's refusal to hit a charmed master (#2375, #2384).
- **Keyword matching** uses the original's rules everywhere. Before, two different matchers disagreed about which item `get black` meant (#2370, #2371).
- `practice` shows the mana cost column (#2270).
- Immortals: `trans all`, OLC's refusal when someone else is editing, and `last` (now showing the saved host) match the original (#2295, #2297, #2317).

**For operators and contributors**

- **The player database gains a `host` column** for `last`. It's added automatically on startup, and existing players read as empty until they next log in (#2317).
- The docs site is now built into darkpawns.org, and the README is rewritten as a guide to the project (#2229, #2230, #2267, #2268).
- The test suite got harder to fool. Scenarios whose setup step quietly failed in both games, so they "passed" without testing anything, are now refused or repaired (#2280, #2331, #2389, #2395). Hundreds of lines of the original's output gained a live witness in the original's own transcript (DP-1502).
- We measured how often the test census notices a one-letter break in a random piece of game output: about half to three fifths of the time in the two packages sampled (#2402). The misses from the previous round are now covered (#2299).

Every fix above was checked against the original C source. The release census ran 6,411 scenario runs against the original game across 16 seeds, and all were clean.

Install on macOS or Linux:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://darkpawns.org/install.sh | bash
```

Existing installations can run `darkpawns update --check`, then `darkpawns update`, or update from **Admin → Updates**.
