# DP-1383 prerequisite: NPC stop-follower state

Zach approved this shared prerequisite after the death-follower audit found
that Go deliberately left CHARM/GROUP set. C src/utils.c:415-416,440-442
removes SPELL_CHARM when charmed, clears the master and unconditionally clears
CHARM and GROUP. Mirror this in StopFollowerMob. A direct ride-charm bit and
a spell record are distinct inputs; both lose the bit, only the spell record
needs affect removal. No RNG added or removed.

TestStopFollowerMobClearsCharmAndGroup checks both forms and the spell record.
TestDamageBreaksMountCharmAndGroup proves the reachable damage()-to-shared-helper
boundary (src/fight.c:1457-1458). The control script removes charm clear,
group clear and spell removal separately; every mutation compiles and fails
its named assertion, with restored green between mutations (R5h).

Other readers: doRide's charm refusal, mob group XP, follower movement and
commands, combatFollowingBody, Lua affect flags, and mob stat output consume
these fields. NPC state is runtime only; no save format change. The shared
helper also runs from stop/attack/leader cleanup. Existing mount unmount and
Act messages retain their ordering. RemoveAffectBySpell uses the mob lock for
its snapshot/update and releases it before AffectTotal. Field setters take
and release their own locks. No World or body lock spans Act delivery.

This prerequisite rides with deferred death cleanup; one combined census at
the train tip covers the shared call sites. Existing charm/shadow notice
visibility differences are outside this state-only prerequisite.
