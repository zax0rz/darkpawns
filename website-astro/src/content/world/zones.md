Dark Pawns spans 95 zones across three continents: **Aralius** (the main continent, home to Kir Drax'in), **Xixieqi** (the southern continent), and the distant lands of **Arakon** and beyond. Zone VNUMs are the starting room numbers for each area.

Zone names prefixed with **(I)** are Immortal-only or heavily restricted. Names prefixed with **(X)** are cross-continent (special access required).

## The Continents

### Aralius (Main Continent)

The primary landmass. Home to **Kir Drax'in** (room 8000+), the largest city and default respawn point. Forests, slums, great plains, and ocean coasts surround the city.

Notable zones: Kir Drax'in (80), Great Forest (91), Slums (70), Guard Training Centre (163), Great Plains (151).

### Xixieqi (Southern Continent)

A harsh southern landscape of sands, savannas, and ocean waters. Accessible via the Moongates (zone 40).

Notable zones: Xixieqi (48), Xixieqi Sands (50), Xixieqi Highlands (52), Sulfur Fur Mountains (47).

### Arakon's Reaches

Distant planes, islands, and dimensional pockets. Many require Immortal approval or special items to enter.

Notable zones: Prison Island (25), Elemental Planes (13), Sky Castle (161), King Solomon's Mines (128).

## Using This Guide with Agents

When navigating programmatically, use the `ROOM_VNUM` variable to determine your current zone:

```python
vnum = variables.get("ROOM_VNUM", 0)
zone_id = vnum // 100  # approximate zone from VNUM
```

Combine with `ROOM_EXITS` to plan movement and `ROOM_MOBS` to assess targets before engaging. The `consider` command evaluates relative power before committing to combat.
