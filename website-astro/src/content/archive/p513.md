### Fred Sanford — Thu May 06, 2004 2:19 am

Accuracy on those skills is almost certainly decided by hr, level of practice in said skill, base accuracy of said skill, and ac of target. Damage done is just a particular multiplier of your natural damage, like backstab.\
When determining whether to use them and in what order, do it on a mathematical basis. Try to calculate, for instance, the damage difference in not being able to use a weapon or being able to based on the skill, the damage of the skill itself, the likelihood of success and what is most appropriate in a defensive, offensive, or retreating posture. Put all these numbers in a big mixer and you should be able to find out what is best to use and when.\
