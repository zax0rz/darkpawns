### aidan — Mon May 03, 2004 1:00 pm

I ran across this link the other day and as you can see, the last advertisement in a newsgroup of dark pawns is 05/05/95 - I'm positive steps have been taken since then but to little effect.\
what I've noticed over the years, however, is that most people don't see an ad and start playing (unless it's a really good ad). most people play because friends of their's play. keeping that in mind, there are a few things we can do as a playerbase that can positively benefit the mud.\
mud forums: if you converse in a mud forum, stick a link in your signature. either point it here, darkpawns.com, or telnet://pawns.wolfpaw.net:4300\
tell your friends! - if you have a friend with a computer who can read, tell them about dp and get them involved. they will be much more likely to play and stay if they already know someone.\
what other creative ways are there to advertise?\

### noogie — Tue May 04, 2004 3:54 pm

Haha, my friend and former next door neighbor "Hornet" got me hooked. I used to make fun of him for text games too, and challenged his manhood for it........all I have to say is curse you for infecting me with this prion-like disease.\
Now to discuss advertising......\
For other muds I have played on or worked on, I find that leaving good reviews on sites such as the Mudconnector can really reel in new players. The hard part is keeping honest with your claims. Hopefully people on DP will have a little more tolerance for new players than in the present and past. As long as I have mudded here the word "newbie" is a definate derrogatory term. Thus, anyone falling in the category of the intended definition of newbie, finds their first days here rather repulsive. A change in attitude by the player base for new people, and realizing they won't log on and automatically be the cream of the crop is much needed if advertisment is to be effective in hooking long-term players. Nobody wants to come to a mud where the general feeling is "nobody deserves to play here but me."\
