On Dark Pawns, [CLANS](/help/commands/clan-clans/) are associations of players that have banded together
for a specific purpose.  Most [CLANS](/help/commands/clan-clans/) are made up of adventurers who have decided
to work together for mutual gain.

If you would like to form your own clan, you need to be over level 10 and enlist
the aid of two other players (not multis) who are currently not in any other clans.
After these requirements have been met, ask to speak to a God.  Additionally, there 
is usually a minor quest or task involved in order to finalize the creation of your
new clan.

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) WHO Displays the names and ranks of fellow clan-members that are currently
	 online.

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) MEMBERS Displays all the members in your clan.

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) STATUS Displays your current rank in your [CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/).

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [INFO](/help/commands/info-news-policy-version-future/) Displays all available information on a named [CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) (type [CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [INFO](/help/commands/info-news-policy-version-future/)
	  without any arguments to get a list of current [CLANS](/help/commands/clan-clans/)).

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [QUIT](/help/commands/quit/) Allows you to leave your clan behind.

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) APPLY Apply to join a named clan, but only if you meet their requirements.

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) ENROLL Allows you to enroll players into your clan. Typing [CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) ENROLL
            without any arguments shows a list of players who have applied to
            join your clan.

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) EXPEL Allows you to kick someone out of your clan. 

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) PROMOTE Promote a player in your clan to a higher rank

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) DEMOTE Demote a player in your clan to a lower rank

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [WITHDRAW](/help/commands/bank-withdraw-deposit-balance/) Make a withdrawal from the clan bank account

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [DEPOSIT](/help/commands/bank-withdraw-deposit-balance/) Deposit into the clan bank account

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [SET](/help/wizhelp/set/) RANKS Set the number of ranks in your clan

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [SET](/help/wizhelp/set/) APPFEE Set the application fee for people to apply for your clan

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [SET](/help/wizhelp/set/) DUES Set the required monthly dues for your clan members

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [SET](/help/wizhelp/set/) APPLEV Set the minimum level a player must be to apply to your clan

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [SET](/help/wizhelp/set/) PLAN Create a description of your clan for all to see

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) PRIVATE Toggle whether or not your clan is private or public

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [SET](/help/wizhelp/set/) PRIVILEGE Set the privileges of the clan commands for clan members

[CLAN](/help/commands/clan-clans/) [SET](/help/wizhelp/set/) [TITLE](/help/commands/title/) Set the title for each rank in your clan

See also: [CTELL](/help/commands/ctell/)
