Usage: give &lt;object&gt; &lt;victim&gt;
       give &lt;number&gt; coins &lt;victim&gt;

If you feel benevolent, you may give some stuff to someone in need.

  > give all ras
  > give bread doctor
  > give all.bronze mirlen
  > give 40000 coins nia
