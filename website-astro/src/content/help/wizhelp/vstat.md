Usage: vstat &lt; mob | obj &gt; &lt;virtual number&gt;

Used to stat a prototypical mobile or object by virtual number.

Examples:

  > vstat obj 3000
  > vstat mob 3001

See also: [LOAD](/help/wizhelp/load/), [STAT](/help/wizhelp/stat/), [VNUM](/help/wizhelp/vnum/)
