### OUTLAW System

Frontline introduced the OUTLAW system for player killing. An outlaw flag is automatically assigned after a successful PK. The flag is permanent, and will not be removed through death or remort of your character.

### The Aiko Story

A player mudding during a lecture had their speakers on and got paged roughly 20 times by in-game notifications, disrupting the entire class.

Moral of the story: Turn your speakers down if you are going to mud during lecture. :)
