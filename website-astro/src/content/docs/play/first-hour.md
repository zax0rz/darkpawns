Start with the game's own directions. Type `help newbie`, `help map`, and `help faq` when you're in the game; they explain the first steps and show Kir Drax'in. You'll find your guild and practice your starting skills wherever your hometown is. If your character starts in Kir Drax'in, follow the route below to its Guard Barracks; other hometowns have their own guild and training routes. You'll probably want more characters soon. [Connect a client](/docs/play/connect-client/) shows how to run the three-character party the game was built around.

> You will start out in the Temple of your home town. The first thing you should do is find your GUILD and PRACTICE your starting skills.
>
> Source: `help NEWBIE`

## Before you start

- A new character in the temple of your chosen hometown.
- Your starting equipment in your inventory.

![A new Guest at the Temple Altar in the web client, with the connection status and vitals below the game text.](/media/docs/web-client-first-steps.png)

## Put on your equipment

1. Type `wear all`.

   The game tries to wear each item it can. Bread goes in your stomach, not on your head.

## Find your guild and practice

1. Type `score` and note your hometown and class.
2. Type `help guild` for the Kir Drax'in guild locations, or ask on newbie for directions in another hometown.
3. Go to the guild for your class and type `practice`.

The GUILD help lists thieves on the southern wall road, warriors on the Street of Swords, clerics inside the Temple of the Cross, mages on the Street of Wizardry, and psionicists and ninjas on the Avenue of the Gods. Guilds are restricted by class. If you need to return to the temple, type `recall`.

## If your hometown is Kir Drax'in: reach the Guard Barracks

Use this route only if your hometown is Kir Drax'in. If it's another city, keep to the local guild and training area; ask on newbie for directions or use `help map`.

1. From the Temple Altar, go south into the Temple of the Cross.
2. Go south again to Temple Square.
3. Go west to the Avenue of the Gods, then north onto the Street of Swords.
4. Go north along the Street of Swords. The barracks entrance is on the west side.
5. Go west into the Guard Barracks, open the south door, then go south into the New Recruit Training Area.

The game calls the room beyond the door the New Recruit Training Area. The FAQ calls the same training area the Guard Training Centre. Type `help map` to see Kir Drax'in's map.

## Try a first fight

1. From the New Recruit Training Area, go east, then north up the stairs.
2. Go down into Kir Drax'in Guard Basic Training - Level One.
3. Type `look` and find a grey guard trainee.
4. Type `consider trainee` to get a rough estimate of the fight.
5. If it looks manageable, type `kill trainee` and watch your health.

The FAQ recommends this training area for experience and warns against fighting the city's mobs. If a fight turns bad, `flee` tries to escape through a suitable exit and may cost experience. Afterward, `score` shows your health and experience. Return to your guild and type `practice` when new skills become available.

## Keep your equipment

There is no rent. To keep your equipment when you quit, quit in a temple or your hometown's quit room. `help newbie` names the Temple and Altar in Kir Drax'in as the main safe place.

## Did it work?

`score` shows your character details, and `practice` lists available skills. If you followed the Kir Drax'in route, you should now be in its Guard Training Centre.

## If it didn't work

- **You can't find the training area.** Type `recall`, then use `help map` and ask on newbie.
- **You're hungry.** The FAQ says `eat` and `carve`; it also points to a bakery east, then north of Market Square.
- **You died in the city.** Newbie help recommends the Guard Barracks and other newbie areas. `recall` returns you to your hometown temple.
