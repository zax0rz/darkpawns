Start by putting on your equipment, finding the guild for your hometown and class, and practicing your starting skills. This route to the guard training area is for Kir Drax'in. Dark Pawns gives new characters enough food to get started, but the help file warns that city fights are a poor place to begin.

## Before you start

- A new character in the Temple of their chosen hometown.
- Your starting equipment in your inventory.

## Put on your equipment

1. Type `wear all`.

   The game tries each item in your inventory. Some things do not belong on a body; bread is the example the help file chose.

## Find your guild and practice

1. Type `score` and note your hometown.
2. Type `help guild` for the guild locations listed for Kir Drax'in. In another hometown, type `newbie` followed by your question to ask for local directions.
3. Go to your class guild and type `practice`.

   Guild access depends on class. The GUILD help lists these Kir Drax'in locations: thieves on the southern wall road, warriors on the Street of Swords, clerics in the Temple of the Cross, mages on the Street of Wizardry, and psionicists and ninjas on the Avenue of the Gods.

## Start in Kir Drax'in

The FAQ directs new players in Kir Drax'in to the Guard Barracks on the Street of Swords, then recommends staying in its training area and the other Kir Drax'in newbie areas while leveling. If your hometown is elsewhere, use its local training area instead; this route does not lead there.

1. If you are at the Temple Altar, go south to the Temple of the Cross.
2. Go south to Temple Square.
3. Go west to the Avenue of the Gods.
4. Go north twice along the Street of Swords.
5. Go west into the Guard Barracks.
6. Open the door south and go south into the New Recruit Training Area.

The game names the entrance New Recruit Training Area; the help file calls it the Guard Training Centre. If you lose your way, type `recall` to return to your hometown temple. Kir Drax'in's map is available with `help map`.

## Try your first fight

1. From the New Recruit Training Area, go east into the next part of the New Recruit Training Area.
2. Go north through the New Recruit Training Area, then down to Kir Drax'in Guard Basic Training - Level One.
3. Type `look`. This floor has grey guard trainees; their short description is “a guard trainee”.
4. Type `consider trainee` for a rough estimate of the fight. The estimate is not a promise.
5. If it looks manageable, type `kill trainee`. Watch your health while combat runs.

The FAQ recommends this training area for experience and levels, and warns new players against fighting the city's mobs. If a fight is going badly, `flee` tries to escape through a suitable exit and can cost experience. After combat, `score` shows your health and experience. Return to your guild and `practice` as skills become available.

## Keep your equipment

There is no rent. To keep your equipment when you quit, quit in a temple or the quit room for your hometown. The Temple and Altar in Kir Drax'in are the main safe place described in `help newbie`.

## Check it worked

Use `score` to check your character and `practice` to see available skills. The area around you should be the Guard Training Centre if you followed the route from the temple.

## If it went wrong

- **You cannot find the training centre.** Type `recall`, then use `help map` and ask on newbie.
- **You are hungry.** The FAQ says `eat` and `carve`; it also points to a bakery one east, one north of Market Square.
- **You died in the city.** Newbie help recommends staying in the Guard Training Centre and other newbie areas. Recall returns you to the temple.
