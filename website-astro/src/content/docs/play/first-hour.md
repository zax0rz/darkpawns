You've made a character and you're standing in a temple with a pack full of starter gear. Here's how to get through the first hour alive. Mostly.

> You will start out in the Temple of your home town. The first thing you should do is find your GUILD and PRACTICE your starting skills.
>
> `help newbie`

## Before you start

- A new character, standing in the temple of your hometown.
- Three commands worth reading right now: `help newbie`, `help map` and `help faq`.

![A new character at the Temple Altar in the web client, with vitals below the game text.](/media/docs/web-client-first-steps.png)

## Gear up

1. Type `wear all`.

   You put on everything you can. Some things don't belong on a body, though. You can't wear bread.

## Find your guild and practice

1. Type `score` to check your class and hometown.
2. Type `help guild` to see where the guilds are in Kir Drax'in.
3. Walk to your class's guild and type `practice`.

In Kir Drax'in, warriors train on the Street of Swords, mages on the Street of Wizardry, clerics inside the Temple of the Cross, thieves on the southern wall road, and psionicists and ninjas on the Avenue of the Gods. Guilds only let in their own class.

Lost? Type `recall` to go back to your temple, or ask for directions with `newbie <your question>`.

## Walk to the Guard Barracks

`help newbie` calls the Guard Barracks in Kir Drax'in "one of the best places to start". From the Temple Altar:

1. Go south into the Temple of the Cross, then south again to Temple Square.
2. Go west to the Avenue of the Gods, then north onto the Street of Swords.
3. Keep going north up the Street of Swords until the Guard Barracks entrance opens to the west.
4. Go west into the Guard Barracks.
5. Open the south door and go south into the New Recruit Training Area.

Started somewhere other than Kir Drax'in? Your city has its own guilds and training grounds. `help map` and the newbie channel will point you there.

## Win your first fight

1. From the New Recruit Training Area, go east, then north up the stairs.
2. Go down into **Kir Drax'in Guard Basic Training - Level One**.
3. Type `look` and find a grey guard trainee.
4. Type `consider trainee` to size it up.
5. If you like your odds, type `kill trainee`, and keep an eye on your hit points.

If it goes badly, type `flee`. It works, but it isn't free:

> Fleeing from battle will also cost you some experience points, but hey, thats what you get for wimping out.
>
> `help flee`

Stay in the training area for a while. The FAQ is blunt about it: "Killing mobs in the city when you are a newbie is NEVER a good idea." When you have new skills to learn, go back to your guild and `practice` again.

## Keep your stuff

Your gear only survives logging off if you quit somewhere safe. The game calls that renting, and it's free, but only in certain places. The Temple and Altar in Kir Drax'in is the main one. When you're done for the night, walk back to a temple or your hometown's quit room and type `quit` there. Your gear will be waiting when you come back.

## Get a party together

You'll want more than one character, and soon. Dark Pawns was built for it: you can run up to three at once, and by the mid levels a lone character has a rough time. [Connect a client](/docs/play/connect-client/) shows how to run all three.

## Did it work?

`score` shows experience you didn't have an hour ago, and `practice` lists skills you've started training. You can walk from the temple to the barracks without looking anything up.

## If it didn't work

- **You can't find the barracks.** Type `recall`, check `help map`, and ask on the newbie channel.
- **You're starving.** You can `eat` what you're carrying, or `carve` a corpse. There's a bakery east, then north of Market Square.
- **You keep dying in the city.** Go back to the training area. See the FAQ quote above.
