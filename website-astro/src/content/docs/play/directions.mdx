Every route below starts at **Market Square** in Kir Drax'in (room `#8046`) unless noted otherwise. Each path is verified step by step against the shipped world files (`lib/world/wld/`), cross-checked with the recovered [2004 dp-players.com directions](/archive/player-page-directions/).

<Aside type="tip" title="Reading speedwalk shorthand">
  A number before a direction repeats that step: `13s4wd` means 13 south, 4 west, then down. Directions are `n` (north), `s` (south), `e` (east), `w` (west), `u` (up), and `d` (down). Routes marked **Waterwalk** cross water sectors.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="About older player direction lists">
  Community speedwalk lists from the 2004 `dp-players.com` site and the 2019 DPReturns revival (`darkpawns.net`) include some paths that do not match the current world graph, such as `14s4wd` for Ender Village (the shipped map uses `13s4wd`) or zones reached through locked portals and custom exits. Only routes verified directly against the server's world files appear above. For full room-by-room exploration, open the [interactive world map](/map/).
