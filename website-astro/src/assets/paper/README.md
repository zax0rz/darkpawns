# Scanned book paper

`book-page.jpg` is the unmodified 960px Wikimedia thumbnail of a blank page from *Armide*, published by Henry Lemoine & Cie in 1902. Internet Archive Book Images supplied the scan; Wikimedia Commons records its rights statement as **no known copyright restrictions**. It is not an AI-generated texture.
