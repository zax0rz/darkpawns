# Dark Pawns Archive publication policy

The Archive preserves public community history recovered from `dp-players.com`,
`darkpawns.com` and public Usenet groups, and collections donated by former
players. Recovery files and contact research
live outside this repository in `~/dp-community-revival/`.

## May be published

- Public forum posts, guides, histories, game logs, selected quotes, and public
  staff or player handles. Public availability alone does not require us to
  republish something.
- Public biographical details only when they are materially relevant to the
  historical record and were intentionally posted by that person.
- Material with a verifiable original URL and Wayback capture. Every Astro
  archive entry records both.
- Public Usenet posts with a preserved Message-ID, newsgroup, date and reviewed
  recovery copy. Crossposts should be represented once with every captured
  Message-ID retained in provenance.
- A collection donated by the player who recorded it, with their written
  permission to publish. Each entry names the collection, its donor, the raw
  file and what was removed. The raw files and the permission stay outside this
  repository in `~/dp-community-revival/`.

## Must remain private

- Email addresses, instant-messenger handles, phone numbers, private messages,
  and other direct contact details.
- Contact leads inferred or assembled from multiple sources.
- Raw recovery data that has not been reviewed for personal information.
- Usenet signatures and quoted headers containing email addresses or personal
  contact URLs. Message-IDs remain because they identify the public record.
- Passwords and anything else typed at a login prompt.
- Private tells, whispers and mail shown in a game log. Each line is replaced
  with a marker such as `[private tell removed]`, so line numbers still match
  the original capture. Public channels (say, gossip, group, clan) stay.

## Editorial rules

- Preserve the authors' words. Correct only extraction artifacts; do not
  silently rewrite spelling, tone, or disputes.
- Curate for historical value and reasonable harm to people who are still
  alive. A Wayback capture may be cited without reproducing its contents.
- Do not republish slurs, sexual material, or contextless humiliation unless it
  is necessary to document something of real historical importance. In a game
  log, a line with a slur or sexual violence is replaced with `[line removed]`,
  so the rest of the capture and its line numbers stay intact.
- Label unknown authors and incomplete captures honestly.
- Do not present an archive capture date as the original publication date.
- A failed or empty Wayback response is an inventory lead, not recovered
  content.
- If a former community member asks for sensitive personal information to be
  removed, remove it promptly while retaining a minimal editorial note where
  needed to explain the historical record.
