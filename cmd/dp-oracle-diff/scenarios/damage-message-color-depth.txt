# player-facing law (src/fight.c:995-1015). On a damaging weapon swing C sends
# the attacker's TO_CHAR line bracketed by CCYEL(ch, C_CMP)/CCNRM(ch, C_CMP) and

# room act() carries no color. Creation answers Y to "Do you want ANSI color"
