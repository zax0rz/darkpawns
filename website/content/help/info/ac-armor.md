"[AC](/help/info/ac-armor/)" is your armor class.

Items often have one of two effects: ac-apply, or "armor". These two are 
inverses of one another; ac-apply 10 = armor -10;
You can get a rough idea of what your current armor class is in [SCORE](/help/commands/score/).

See also: [SCORE](/help/commands/score/)
