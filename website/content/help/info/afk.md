Usage: afk

[AFK](/help/info/afk/) means "away from keyboard". Use this command when you're going afk to
get a beer, smoke a cigarette, etc. It simply lets people who [TELL](/help/commands/say-tell-ask-whisper-shout-holler/) to you
know you are [AFK](/help/info/afk/) and might not get the message. Type [AFK](/help/info/afk/) again to toggle
it off.
