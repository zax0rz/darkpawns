You are an [OUTLAW](/help/info/outlaw-outlaws/) on Dark Pawns if you have pkilled or stolen from another
player.  Outlaws are generally not well liked and are known to be hunted
to the death by many blood-thirsty and vengeful adventurers.

Note: A player who wishes to become an [OUTLAW](/help/info/outlaw-outlaws/) should think long and hard
about the choice, as the consequences for being an [OUTLAW](/help/info/outlaw-outlaws/) are as severe as
one might expect.

See also: [PK](/help/info/pk-player-killing-pkill-pkilling-pkiller/), [PSTEAL](/help/info/psteal-player-stealing-pstealing/)
