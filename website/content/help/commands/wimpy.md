Usage: wimpy [hp]

Use the [WIMPY](/help/commands/wimpy/) command to automatically flee once your health drops below
a certain level. Note that you can't flee if you are not standing.
[WIMPY](/help/commands/wimpy/) will also trigger [ESCAPE](/help/commands/escape-of-the-mongoose-escape/) or [RETREAT](/help/commands/retreat/), if you possess those abilities.

Examples:

  > wimpy 35    will make your character automatically flee a fight
                if you have less than 35 hit points.
  > wimpy 0     will turn off wimpy mode.
  > wimpy       will show you your current wimp status.

See also: [FLEE](/help/commands/flee/), [TOGGLE](/help/commands/toggle/), [ESCAPE](/help/commands/escape-of-the-mongoose-escape/), [RETREAT](/help/commands/retreat/)
