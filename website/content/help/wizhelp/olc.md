Usage: olc [save]

Lists the olc'd areas that need saving. With the 'save' option, it saves
all areas that need saving.

See also: [REDIT](/help/wizhelp/redit-sedit-oedit-medit-zedit-tedit/), [SEDIT](/help/wizhelp/redit-sedit-oedit-medit-zedit-tedit/), [OEDIT](/help/wizhelp/redit-sedit-oedit-medit-zedit-tedit/), [MEDIT](/help/wizhelp/redit-sedit-oedit-medit-zedit-tedit/), [ZEDIT](/help/wizhelp/redit-sedit-oedit-medit-zedit-tedit/), [TEDIT](/help/wizhelp/redit-sedit-oedit-medit-zedit-tedit/), [SET](/help/wizhelp/set/)
